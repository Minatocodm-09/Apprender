❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Apprender

🚀 Getting Started

Is Apprender completely free to use?

Yes! Apprender is 100% free to use. You can play unlimited quizzes across all 10+ categories without any registration, subscription, or payment required. We support our platform through non-intrusive advertising to keep it free for everyone. There are no hidden fees, premium tiers, or paywalls – all features including achievements, leaderboards, and quiz categories are completely free.

Do I need to create an account to play quizzes?

No account is required! You can start playing quizzes immediately without any registration process. Simply visit our homepage, choose a category, select your difficulty level, and start playing. However, please note that your progress, achievements, and scores are saved locally in your browser using localStorage technology. This means your data won't transfer if you switch devices or clear your browser data.

Can I play quizzes on mobile devices?

Absolutely! Apprender is fully responsive and works seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. The interface automatically adapts to your screen size for the best experience on any device. You can also install Apprender as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on your mobile device for quick access and offline functionality. Just tap "Add to Home Screen" when prompted by your browser.

🎮 Gameplay

How many questions are in each quiz?

Each quiz contains 10 carefully selected questions. Questions are randomly selected from our extensive database each time you play, so you can retake quizzes and encounter different questions for continuous learning. This randomization ensures that you can practice as many times as you want without memorizing answers. Our question pool is regularly updated with new content.

What are the different difficulty levels?

Each category offers three difficulty levels:

  • Easy: Beginner-friendly questions that test basic knowledge. Perfect for casual learning or warm-up.
  • Medium: Moderate challenge requiring general knowledge and some specific expertise in the topic.
  • Hard: Expert-level questions designed for serious learners and subject matter enthusiasts.

You can choose the level that best matches your current knowledge and gradually progress to harder difficulties as you improve.

How much time do I have to answer each question?

You have 15 seconds to answer each question. A countdown timer is displayed at the top of the quiz screen. If time runs out before you select an answer, the question will be marked as incorrect, and the correct answer will be shown before moving to the next question. This time limit adds an element of challenge and helps improve quick thinking skills.

Can I pause or stop a quiz in progress?

Once you start a quiz, it's designed to be completed in one session (approximately 2-3 minutes total). However, you can exit at any time by clicking the exit button, which will prompt a confirmation dialog. If you exit mid-quiz, your progress won't be saved, and you'll need to start over. We recommend completing quizzes in one go for the best experience and accurate timing statistics.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is calculated based on the number of correct answers out of 10 total questions, displayed as both a raw score (e.g., 7/10) and a percentage (e.g., 70%). Each question has equal weight. Your final results page shows your score, percentage, performance analysis, and personalized feedback to help you improve. Scores are automatically saved to your browser's local storage and count toward achievement unlocks and leaderboard rankings.

⭐ Features & Progress

How are my scores and achievements saved?

Your quiz scores, achievements, statistics, and leaderboard rankings are saved locally in your browser using localStorage technology. This means your progress is completely private and stored only on your device – we don't collect or store any personal data on our servers. The downside is that if you clear your browser cache, switch browsers, or use a different device, your progress won't be available. To maintain your progress, avoid clearing browser data or use the same browser consistently.

How can I earn achievements?

Achievements are unlocked automatically as you complete quizzes and reach various milestones. There are 15 unique achievements including:

  • Completing your first quiz (Getting Started)
  • Achieving perfect scores (Perfect Score, Perfectionist)
  • Playing multiple categories (Category Explorer, Knowledge Seeker)
  • Completing quizzes on different days (Dedicated Learner)
  • Sharing your results (Social Sharer)
  • Time-based achievements (Early Bird, Night Owl, Speed Demon)

Check the Achievements page to see all available badges, your current progress, and which achievements you've unlocked. Pop-up notifications appear when you unlock new achievements.

How do leaderboards work?

Leaderboards track top scores for each category and difficulty level. When you complete a quiz, your score is automatically added to the relevant leaderboard. You're assigned a randomly generated Player ID for anonymous competition. Rankings are based on percentage scores (highest to lowest), with earlier completions winning in case of ties. You can view global leaderboards or filter by specific categories and difficulty levels. Note that leaderboards are stored locally in your browser, so they reflect scores from your device only.

Can I share my quiz results on social media?

Yes! After completing any quiz, you'll see social sharing buttons on your results page. You can share your score directly to Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn. Each share includes your score, the quiz category, difficulty level, and a challenge message to friends. You can also copy a direct link to share via any platform. Sharing your results unlocks the "Social Sharer" achievement!

📚 Content & Categories

What quiz categories are available?

Apprender offers 10 diverse quiz categories:

  • 🎯 General Knowledge – Broad topics and trivia
  • 🔬 Science – Biology, chemistry, physics, and more
  • 💻 Technology – Computing, gadgets, innovations

Each category has Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels with hundreds of questions.

How often are new questions added?

We regularly update our question database to keep content fresh, accurate, and relevant. Our automated system refreshes quiz questions periodically, and we continuously add new questions based on user feedback, current events, and emerging topics. We also review and update existing questions to ensure accuracy and remove outdated information. Check back frequently for new challenges and updated content!

What if I find an incorrect answer?

We strive for accuracy in all our questions and answers, but errors can occasionally occur. If you believe you've found an incorrect answer, outdated information, or a misleading question, please contact us through our Contact page. Include details about the specific question, the category, difficulty level, and why you believe the answer is incorrect. We review all reports promptly and update questions as needed. Your feedback helps us maintain high-quality content for all users!

🔒 Privacy & Safety

Is my personal information collected or stored?

No! Apprender does not require registration, so we don't collect names, email addresses, or any personal information. All your quiz data (scores, achievements, statistics) is stored locally in your browser and never transmitted to our servers. We use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics (page views, popular categories) to improve our service, but this data cannot be linked to individual users.

Is Apprender safe for children?

Yes! Apprender is family-friendly and appropriate for users of all ages. Our quiz content is educational and does not contain inappropriate language, violence, or adult themes. We display non-intrusive advertisements that comply with family-safe advertising standards. However, we recommend parental supervision for younger children, especially when using the internet. If you encounter any inappropriate content, please report it to us immediately through our Contact page.
