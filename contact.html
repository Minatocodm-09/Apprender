How Apprender Support Works

Our team reviews support requests in the order they are received and prioritizes issues that block quiz access, score tracking, or account functionality. For the fastest response, include your device type, browser, and a brief description of what happened before the issue appeared.

We typically respond to most queries within one to two business days. Suggestions for new quiz categories, question corrections, and accessibility improvements are reviewed weekly by our editorial and product teams.

Common Quiz Issues and Fast Fixes

If a quiz does not load, first refresh the page and verify that JavaScript is enabled in your browser. If scores are not updating, clear stale site data for Apprender and try a fresh session. Mobile users should also confirm they are not in low-data mode, which can block script execution.

For repeated errors, share the quiz category, difficulty level, and approximate time of the issue. This helps us match your report against server logs and resolve technical problems faster.

Partnership & Content Submission Guidelines

We welcome collaboration requests for educational content, branded quiz campaigns, and academic partnerships. Please include your organization name, project scope, and timeline in your message so we can route your request to the right team.

If you are reporting a question correction, include the exact question, current answer, suggested replacement, and a trusted source. This helps our editors validate and publish updates quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Apprender really free?

Yes! Apprender is completely free to use. We support our platform through advertisements.

Do I need to create an account?

No registration required! Simply visit our website and start playing instantly.

How often are new quizzes added?

We regularly update our quiz database with new questions and categories. Check back often for fresh content!

Absolutely! We welcome suggestions. Email us at contact@apprender.com with your ideas.
